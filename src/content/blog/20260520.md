Thanks for visiting my blog!

In the realm of technical topics, I like to write about programming languages and systems: build systems, operating systems, and concurrency. There's a quote that I like, often (and perhaps apocryphally) attributed to Richard Feynman: "If you can't explain it to a freshman, you don't really understand it." The goal of my writing is to make difficult concepts accessible to any engineer with a baseline understanding of programming and computer science. I believe this to be a testament to how much I really understand the topics I write about.

Lastly, like other recent interests in my life, writing offers a channel to address my fears and manage a healthy balance between perfectionism and progress. (I had previously added bouldering to this category, but I realise that I may just be deathly afraid of falling.) Writing has long been pivotal in my learning and perception of the world, but in recent years, I've been less intentional in this practice. In an age where the cost of generation is cheaper than ever, it is important that my judgement and ability to convey ideas effectively does not atrophy. This blog doubles as a way to do so.

P.S. In the spirit of readability I've given up on the retro font. To my merit, Apple did too.
