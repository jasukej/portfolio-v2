Thanks for visiting my blog!

In the realm of technical topics, I like to write about programming languages and systems: build systems, operating systems, and concurrency. There's a quote that I like to keep in mind, often (and perhaps apocryphally) attributed to Richard Feynman: "If you can't explain it to a freshman, you don't really understand it." The goal of my writing is to make difficult concepts accessible to any engineer with a baseline understanding of programming and computer science. I believe this to be a testament to how much I really understand the topics I write about.

Lastly, like other recent interests in my life, this blog offers a channel to address my fears and manage a healthy balance between perfectionism and progress. (I had previously added bouldering to this category, but I realise that I may just be deathly afraid of falling.) Writing has long been pivotal in my learning and perception of the world, but it has been a challenge to internalize that things do not have to be perfect before they are shared.

I hope you enjoy reading these as much as I enjoyed writing them :)
