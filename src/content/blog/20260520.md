Thanks for visiting my blog!

In the realm of technical topics, I like to write about programming languages and systems: build systems, operating systems, and concurrency. There's a quote that I like to keep in mind, often (and perhaps apocryphally) attributed to Feynman: "If you can't explain it to a freshman, you don't really understand it." I hope my writing is accessible to any engineer with a baseline understanding of programming and computer science, as I believe this to be a testament to how much I really understand the topics I write about.

Like many other recent interests in my life (cough: bouldering), this blog is also a way for me to address my fears and manage a healthy balance between perfectionism and progress. Put simply, I write to overcome the idea that things must be perfect and polished before they are shared.

I hope you enjoy reading these as much as I enjoyed writing them :)
